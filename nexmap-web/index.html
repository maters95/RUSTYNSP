What changes in my map?

Only password-protection fields and the map integrity hash are updated. Other archive files, including your resources, are preserved. The output is a new AES-256-encrypted .nexmap archive compatible with NSP.

Is anything uploaded or stored?

No. Processing happens in memory on your device. There are no accounts, analytics, external fonts, or file-upload endpoints. Closing this page clears the working copy; downloaded files remain wherever you save them.

What should I check before using it?

Keep a backup and inspect the downloaded map in NSP before use. This tool recovers access; it does not validate calibration safety, change ECU security, or connect to your vehicle. Only use files you own or are authorised to modify.
